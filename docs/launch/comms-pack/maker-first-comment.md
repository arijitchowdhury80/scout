# Product Hunt First Maker Comment

Voice: plain, sharp, first-person founder. No em dashes. No hype. The single most important text on launch day.

---

Hi Product Hunt. I'm Arijit, and Scout is the thing I've wanted to exist for years.

Here's the honest origin. Almost every piece of research I've ever written had the same crack running through it. I'd pull facts off websites, drop them into a doc, and move on. Weeks later someone would ask "wait, where did this come from," and I couldn't answer. I'd built something on a foundation I couldn't point to. That's not research. That's guessing in a nice font.

The tools I reached for made it worse, not better. Point them at a site and you get a giant wall of text. You still have to read it, clean it, and manually track which page said what. The tool did the easy part and handed me the hard part.

So I built Scout to do the opposite.

You point it at a website and it gives you records, not a blob. For a company, that's the company details, the people, the open roles, the funding history, and the recent news, each one in its own clean field. It also does the everyday jobs I kept writing throwaway scripts for: read a single page, crawl a whole site, list every URL on a domain, grab a full-page screenshot, and turn an online store into search-ready product records.

The part I care about most: every fact keeps a link back to the exact page it came from. When you hand the output to a client, a teammate, or an AI model, you can show your work. That's what I mean by evidence-grade. Nothing floats free of its source.

You can use it two ways. A simple web API you can call from anything. And a skill that plugs straight into Claude and Codex, so your assistant can go read the web and bring back real records instead of making things up.

Now the ask. This is a beta, and I mean that word. I'm opening 50 spots. I want 50 people who work with web data day to day to use it hard and tell me the truth. I want to know which sites break it, which features you reach for, and which ones you ignore. The blunt feedback is the entire point of launching this way instead of hiding until it's "perfect."

I'll be here all day answering everything. Point Scout at a website you know well and tell me what you think. And if it falls over, tell me that first. That's the most useful thing you can give me today.

Thank you for taking a look.
